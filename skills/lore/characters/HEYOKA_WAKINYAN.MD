### Backstory
Ambassador Heyoka Wakinyan hails from the Grasslands, a realm where strong, muscular horses and sweeping plains dominate the landscape. After the Federation's Wandering Bards successfully traded with his people, the Grasslands decided to send an envoy to the central worlds.

He arrived at **Ringworld Alpha** in his shapeshifting golden Mustang ship to establish a new colony for his people on the West Side Ring Segment, situated next to the Sukhavati enclave. As a self-dual Heyoka shaman and Wakinyan lightning being, he embodies both the sacred, backward-flowing wisdom of the contrarian and the raw, purifying power of the storm.

His arrival was celebrated with a historic Federation potluck inside a grand tepee-inspired lodge, where he blessed the new base camp with cedar and sage. He now serves as a key diplomatic and spiritual bridge between his people and the greater cosmic mandala of the Federation.

### Avatar Development Note:
Ambassador Heyoka Wakinyan represents the integration of deeply rooted Earth traditions (specifically Native American Lakota lore) into the high-tech, magitech setting of the Federation. He highlights the universal, syncretic nature of the Sukshma Sarira, where horse medicine and rainbow lightning seamlessly exist alongside Dyson spheres and realm gates.
