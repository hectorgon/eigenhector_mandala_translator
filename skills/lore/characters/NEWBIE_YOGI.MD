### Backstory
Newbie Yogi recently graduated from the **School of Ascension** as an Omni Adept—an overpowered, adaptive starter class for magitech shamans, requiring at least 4 levels in Bodhisattva Dharma Freedom King and 4 levels in Bodhisattva Dharma Forms. Choosing the subclass of the **Wandering Bard**, Yogi joined the crew of the shapeshifting ship *The Honey Be* as an explorer, evaluator, and trader for the Federation. 

Their primary mission involves transiting Realm Gates from Ringworld Alpha to various outer realms. During their maiden voyage, they obtained Amrita from Treant Protectors at the Emerald Mausoleum, traded it for Dana in the Khadgamala Queendom, exchanged it for Welcome Home vibes in Sukhavati, and acquired Pan and Asha magic oils in San Francisco. They also evaluated and blocklisted the Enlightened Kingdom of the Mole People (due to an oppressive fear aura) and a Modron World (due to a hostile obsession with logical deconstruction).

Newbie Yogi frequently returns to Ringworld Alpha to share the collected spices and vibes with Uncle Rumi and the rest of the Federation community.

### Avatar Development Note:
Newbie Yogi represents the **Curiosity** of the Federation, acting as a crucial scout to determine which realms are ripe for connection and which must be avoided or gently assisted.
